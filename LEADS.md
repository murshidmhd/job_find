# ✉️ Outreach Templates (Cold DMs)

Use these templates to reach out to these prospects. **Remember**: Personalize the first line!

## Template A: Fashion & Boutique (e.g., Grace Boutique, Kathy's)
**Subject/Opener**: Love the latest collection! 😍

"Hey [Name/Brand], I’ve been following your page for a while and your latest collection is stunning! 👗 

With [Number] followers, I noticed you're managing all orders via WhatsApp/DM. That must be a lot of manual work! I’m a web developer from Kerala and I help boutiques like yours automate sales with a **professional e-commerce site**.

A website would:
1. Let customers buy 24/7 without waiting for your reply.
2. Protect your brand if anything ever happens to your IG account.
3. Show up on Google searches for '[Category] in Kochi'.

Would you be open to a 5-minute chat about how this could save you 10+ hours a week? No pressure at all!"

## Template B: Home Decor & Interiors (e.g., Kerala Home Dreamers)
**Subject/Opener**: Your designs are next level! 🏠

"Hi [Name], your interior projects are absolutely beautiful—that [Specific Project] you posted recently was top-tier!

I noticed you don't have a dedicated portfolio website yet. For a high-end brand like yours, a website isn't just for sales; it's a **digital showroom** that builds massive trust with premium clients.

I specialize in building sleek, fast portfolios for designers. I'd love to show you a quick mockup of how your work could look on a dedicated '.in' domain. 

Are you free for a quick call sometime this week?"

## Template C: Food & Beverage (e.g., Mocha Art Cafe)
**Subject/Opener**: Best vibes in town! ☕

"Hey Team [Cafe Name]! Always love the vibe at your spot. 

I was looking for your digital menu/booking link today but couldn't find a website. I’m a local developer and I build **smart restaurant sites** that include digital menus and easy table reservations.

It’s a great way to reduce the phone calls/DMs and give your customers a smoother experience before they even arrive.

Would love to drop by and show you what's possible! Who is the best person to speak with?"

---
**Strategy**: Reach out via Instagram DM or visit their Kochi locations. Mention how a website can protect their business from algorithm changes and increase sales automation.
